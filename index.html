Our Story

Education Built on
Passion & Precision

Esmè Academy was founded by Coralee — a certified lash artist and esthetician with 7 years of hands-on industry experience and 2 years of dedicated teaching. Based in Yorkton, Saskatchewan, her mission is simple: to provide the kind of education she wished she'd had starting out. Intensive, honest, and built around real results.

Every course is taught personally by Coralee. Whether you're joining in-person or learning on your own time virtually, you get the same dedicated support, quality materials, and certification. Flexible formats designed around your life.
